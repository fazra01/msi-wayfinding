How to use

Floors: pick a floor top-left. Nodes you place belong to that floor; load a map image per floor.

Cross-floor links: give matching stairs/escalator/elevator on two floors the same connector ID (e.g. blue-stairs).

Test Route: hides the graph and shows only the route, a You-Are-Here marker and a destination marker. Use Continue to next floor to follow floor changes. Clear Route restores editing.
